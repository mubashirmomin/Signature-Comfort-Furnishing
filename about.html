Who We Are

Mastering the Art of Upholstery

Located in the heart of Kondwa, Pune, Signature Comfort Manufacturing is a premier destination for bespoke furniture creation and meticulous restoration.

Led by Asif Sayyed, our dedicated team specializes in breathing life back into aging fabrics and frames, transforming everyday living spaces into sanctuaries of luxury.

With a quarter-century of dedicated service in the manufacturing industry, we have perfected the delicate balance of combining traditional, time-honored workmanship with the crisp demands of modern aesthetics.
